CASTLE GRIMHOLD - 3D assets, sounds & licences
===============================================

Weapon models (assets/models/*.fbx, embedded in assets/models.js)
All sources are CC0 1.0 (public domain) from OpenGameArt.org. They were
re-oriented, scaled to real-world size, cleaned (loose props removed),
UV-unwrapped where needed, textures downsized to 1024/512 px and
re-exported as binary FBX 7.4 with embedded textures (Blender 3.6).

  rifle.fbx     Lee-Enfield No.4 - "Bolt action Rifle: Lee Enfield" by Lucian Pavel (CC0)
                https://opengameart.org/content/bolt-action-rifle-lee-enfield
  revolver.fbx  "Old Revolver" by Lucian Pavel (CC0)
                https://opengameart.org/content/old-revolver
  flamer.fbx    "Flamethrower PBR (Unity) CC0" by Lucian Pavel (CC0)
                https://opengameart.org/content/flamethrower-pbrunity-cc0
  machete.fbx   "PBR Machete (CC0)" by Lucian Pavel (CC0)
                https://opengameart.org/content/pbr-machete-cc0
  sten.fbx      "Sten Smg" by ege (CC0)  (converted from .blend; magazine seated in its
                housing, new UVs and a baked parkerised-steel texture with AO / worn edges)
                https://opengameart.org/content/sten-smg
  grenade.fbx   "Stielhandgranate" by elmerenges (CC0)  (converted from .blend; baked
                texture: painted head, wooden handle, steel cap)
                https://opengameart.org/content/stielhandgranate
  Suppressors are built in code.

First-person arms (assets/models/arms.fbx)
  "fps arms (rigged only)" by para (CC0, made with MakeHuman)
  https://opengameart.org/content/fps-arms-rigged-only
  Bones renamed, wool battledress sleeves + cuffs baked into the texture (Blender 3.6).
  Posed in code with two-bone IK onto each weapon's grips, fingers curled per grip.

German soldiers (assets/models/german.fbx)
  "Rigged Lowpoly WW2 Soldier" by nisu (CC0)
  https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-lowpoly-ww2-soldier
  Helmet joined into the body mesh. Uniform variants (Wehrmacht, SS, officer, sniper
  camo, undead) are repainted at runtime; caps are generated in code.

Animations / boss (loaded from assets/characters.js, three.js examples, MIT / Mixamo)
  Soldier.glb  https://threejs.org/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
               (its Idle / Walk / Run clips are retargeted onto the German soldier)
  Xbot.glb     https://threejs.org/examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb  (the Übersoldat)

Sound effects (assets/sounds/*.mp3, embedded in assets/sounds.js), all CC0
Gunshots are synthesised in code (Web Audio). Samples were trimmed, peak-normalised
and converted to mono MP3.
  Kenney "RPG Audio" (doors, latches, clicks, cloth, knife, coins)
                https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
  Kenney "Impact Sounds" (footsteps stone/wood, body hits, metal, thuds)
                https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  "grunts of male death and pain" (CC0, OpenGameArt)
                https://opengameart.org/content/grunts-male-death-and-pain
  "Zombies Sound Pack" (CC0, OpenGameArt)
                https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack
  "25 CC0 bang / firework SFX" (explosions, CC0, OpenGameArt)
                https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx
  "Fire Crackling" (CC0, OpenGameArt)
                https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling
  "Sirens and Alarm Noise" (CC0, OpenGameArt)
                https://opengameart.org/content/sirens-and-alarm-noise

Optional web textures (Poly Haven, CC0) and three.js example textures are
used when reachable; otherwise procedural textures are painted at start-up.

Voice-over (assets/voices/*.mp3, embedded in assets/voices.js)
Generated offline with Piper neural TTS (https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) by
tools/make_voices.py; trimmed, pitched per character, radio filter for London, MP3 mono.
  cs_CZ-jirka-medium              B. J., London (Czech)          dataset licence: CC0
  de_DE-thorsten-high             guard, soldier voice C (German) Thorsten-Voice, CC0
  de_DE-thorsten_emotional-medium officers, soldier voice A        Thorsten-Voice, CC0
  de_DE-karlsson-low              Dr. Zeman, soldier voice B       M-AILABS Speech Dataset
  Voices: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices

Photo textures for the outdoor missions (loaded at runtime when online, CC0, Poly Haven)
  forest_floor, forrest_ground_01, leafy_grass, grass_ground, muddy_tracks, brown_mud_02,
  gravel_floor, gravel, rock_face, mossy_rock, pine_bark, bark_brown_01,
  weathered_plank_siding, weathered_planks, roof_slates_02, grey_roof_01,
  concrete_wall_005, concrete_layers, corrugated_iron     https://polyhaven.com/
  Every one has a procedural fallback painted in code. Trees, tents, towers, trains,
  rockets, searchlights and the other outdoor props are built in code.
